What you get

The checks a good account manager would run every morning

Uptime and response time

Every site checked every 5 minutes from the outside. Two failures in a row and you get an email; another when it is back.

SSL expiry

Certificate end dates for every custom domain, with a warning 14 days before. Catches DNS changes that broke renewal.

Unpublished changes

Designer edits that have been waiting more than 7 days, so a half-finished change never goes live by accident, and finished work does.

Form submissions

Submissions per form for the last 7 and 30 days. A form that goes quiet after being active gets flagged; broken forms are the most expensive silent failure.

CMS limits

Item counts per collection against the site's plan limit, before the client hits the wall mid-campaign.

SEO basics

Missing or duplicate meta titles, missing descriptions, titles over 60 and descriptions over 160 characters, per page.

Weekly summary

Every Monday, one email with every site's uptime, submissions, SEO issues and flags. Two minutes to read.

Client report

A white-label page per site with your agency name: uptime, forms, publish state, SEO health for the last 30 days. Send the link, done.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask first

Does Flowdeck change anything on my sites?

No. It asks Webflow for read-only access (sites, pages, forms, CMS) and never writes. Uptime is checked from the outside like any visitor would.

Which sites can I connect?

Any site you can see in Webflow, across all your workspaces. Connect once per workspace and pick the sites on Webflow's authorization screen.

Can clients see the dashboard?

Clients get the report link for their own site only. The dashboard is yours.

What data do you keep?

Site metadata, check results for 30 days, submission counts (never the submitted content), page titles and descriptions. Delete your account from Settings and everything goes with it.

Is this made by Webflow?

No. Flowdeck is an independent app built on the Webflow Data API.
